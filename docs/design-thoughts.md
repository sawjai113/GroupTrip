## Draft: Cohesive Frame (2026-08-20) — v0.1, very much a draft

**Working idea:** *A trip with people you like is a story you're still writing. Wanderaid is the calm place where that story lives — and every section answers one of the questions a real trip keeps asking.*

Each section = one question, one job:

| Section | Answers | Job | Energy |
|---|---|---|---|
| Home / Dashboard | "What's happening with my trips?" | The cover + table of contents — what needs me, what I owe, what's current/next | *Now* |
| Current Trip | "What is this trip?" | The anchor — the photo, the dates, the doors into each room | *This is it* |
| Places | "Where should we go?" | The shortlist — the "maybe" pile, light notes, decide later | *Maybe / soon* |
| Itinerary / Planning | "What are we doing, when?" | The timeline — the spine of the trip taking shape, day by day | *Sequence* |
| People | "Who's in this?" | The circle — who's coming, who's organizing, who's who | *Together* |
| Money | "Who owes what?" | The ledger — settle up without friction or a spreadsheet | *Fair & settled* |
| Invite / Join | "Come along." | The threshold — the calm, safe way people enter | *Welcome* |

**Cohesive devices** (what makes it feel like one app, not five features):

1. **One question per screen.** Every screen can say which trip question it answers. If a screen answers two, it splits.
2. **One voice.** Calm, plain, warm — no jargon. The copy rhythm is consistent everywhere ("Tap a card to manage that part of the trip.").
3. **One visual grammar.** Editorial small-caps headers, forest-green actions, hairline cards, serif moments only for the emotional numbers (money, names, dates).
4. **One time posture per section.** Dashboard = now. Places = maybe. Itinerary = sequence. Money = settled. People = together.
5. **The story thread.** Dashboard is the cover, each trip is a chapter, each section is a page — a reader always knows where they are in the story.

**Open questions** (where your thoughts would help most):
- If Wanderaid were a physical object, what would it be? (A notebook? A bulletin board? A campfire? A map table?)
- What's the ONE feeling you want someone to have when they open it? (Calm? Excited? Prepared?)
- Which section do you most want to feel "right" first? (M4 ordering)
- One sentence for the app's personality: "the quiet friend who keeps the trip on track" — or something else?
