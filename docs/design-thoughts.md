## Draft: Cohesive Frame — v0.2 (2026-08-20, after user answers)

**Working idea:** *A trip with people you like is a story you're still writing — dreamed before, lived during, told after. Wanderaid is one calm place for the whole story: every tool a real trip needs, and every section answers one question the trip keeps asking.*

### The trip lifecycle (the spine)

| Phase | The trip | Wanderaid's job | The feeling to create |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Before** | A story being dreamed | Planning together is *fun* — shortlists, timelines, people, money decisions as shared excitement | Excited to go |
| **During** | A story being lived | Keep it organized and easy to track so nobody does logistics-stress | Free to enjoy |
| **After** | A story being told | Close out the logistics easily + a place to share memories and thoughts | Proud / warm closure |

**Principle: the trip is the star, not the app.** The user should feel excited *about the trip* — the app gets out of the way. Every screen serves the trip; the app is invisible glue.

**Personality (draft):** "Keeps the trip on track, but fun and collaborative." The friend who's got the plan AND makes planning feel like part of the fun — organized, warm, playful-but-calm. (Not "quiet friend" — fun matters as much as calm.)

### The sections (unchanged from v0.1, plus the lifecycle lens)

| Section | Answers | Job | Lifecycle home |
|---|---|---|---|
| Home / Dashboard | "What's happening with my trips?" | The cover — what needs me, what I owe, what's current/next | All phases (the landing page; **priority #1**) |
| Current Trip | "What is this trip?" | The anchor — photo, dates, doors into each room | All phases |
| Places | "Where should we go?" | The shortlist — the "maybe" pile | Before (mostly) |
| Itinerary / Planning | "What are we doing, when?" | The timeline — the spine taking shape | Before / During |
| People | "Who's in this?" | The circle — roles and identity | Before / During |
| Money | "Who owes what?" | The ledger — settle without friction | During / After |
| Invite / Join | "Come along." | The threshold — the calm way in | Before |
| *(New direction)* Memories | "Remember when…?" | The after-story — share thoughts + memories after the trip | After |

### Unresolved: the object metaphor

User: "I'm trying to merge all the tools I use to plan a trip into one place. Maybe a toolbox, but that's the wrong feel." — A toolbox = work; this should feel like anticipation. Candidates to test:
- A **shared journal** (the story, written together — calm, personal)
- A **basecamp / expedition wall** (everything pinned in one place — organized, adventurous)
- A **trip captain** (the app as the friend who holds the plan — warm, reliable)
- A **playbook** (one place, but play — fun + practical)
- A **map table** (all the trip on one surface — collaborative, tactile)

### Cohesion rules (from v0.1, still standing)

1. One question per screen — a screen answering two questions splits.
2. One voice — calm, plain, warm; no jargon; consistent rhythm.
3. One visual grammar — editorial small-caps headers, forest actions, hairline cards, serif only for emotional numbers (money, names, dates).
4. One time-posture per section (dashboard = now, places = maybe, itinerary = sequence, money = fair, people = together).
5. The story thread — dashboard = cover, trip = chapter, section = page; the reader always knows where they are.
6. **NEW: excited about the trip, never about the app.**
