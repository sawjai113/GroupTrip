# Wanderaid — Product Brief

**Date:** 2026-08-20 · **Status:** v1 — distilled from the design-thoughts inbox (docs/design-thoughts.md)

## One line

**A trip with people you like is a story you're still writing — dreamed before, lived during, told after. Wanderaid is the trip's basecamp: one calm, collaborative space where the whole story lives.**

## The basecamp

Not a toolbox (one person, for work) — a **basecamp**: built by everyone, for the adventure. Anyone can contribute; anyone can just walk through and see what's going on.

## The trip lifecycle

| Phase | The trip is… | Wanderaid's job | The feeling |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Before** | A story being dreamed | Planning together is *fun* | Excited to go |
| **During** | A story being lived | Organized, easy to track | Free to enjoy |
| **After** | A story being told | Easy closeout + shared memories | Proud / warm closure |

## The seven rooms

| Room | Answers | Core idea |
|---|---|---|
| **Home** | "What's going on across my trips?" | User lens: ticking countdown to the next trip, action-needed, grouped feed, your money |
| **Welcome Desk** | "What is this trip?" | Trip lens: the lobby — this trip's countdown, who's going, group chat, activity feed, invite |
| **Map Wall** | "Where should we go?" | Collaborative POI shortlist — everyone adds, votes (yes / weak-yes / no), pins = on everyone's radar, 🔒 locked-in = booked |
| **Schedule Board** | "What are we doing, when?" | Everyone's flights, stays, and prebooked activities overlaid so the group coordinates around each other |
| **Crew** | "Who's in this?" | Dorm hall + person rooms: their places, expenses, bookings, votes, role, arrival/departure |
| **Kitty** | "Who owes what?" | The fair ledger — quick on-the-go adds, balances, and easy "pay Sam $42 → done" repayment |
| **Journal** | "Remember when…?" | The guest book — one-liners to short paragraphs, album links, chat tie-in. No ratings: it's friends |

## Cross-cutting layers

1. **Tags + participants on every item** — one tag vocabulary (food / hotel / flight / show / museum / custom…); every item carries who's in on it. *Participating ≠ added-by.*
2. **Grouped feeds** — "Sam added 15 places" = one line, not a flood.
3. **Call-for-vote / call-for-input** — any item can ask the group ("want feedback on this POI?", "anyone else in this hotel room?") → unanswered calls surface in Home's **action-needed**.

## Principles

1. One question per screen — a screen answering two splits.
2. Glance first, contribute second — works for lurkers and contributors.
3. The trip is the star, never the app.
4. One voice: calm, plain, warm, no jargon.
5. Serif moments only for the emotional numbers — money, names, dates, the countdown.
6. Excited about the trip, not the app.

## First-release scope (decided)

Basic in-app chat (trip channel + cross-trip DMs) · live countdowns · grouped activity feeds · action-needed · weighted POI votes · bookings on the schedule board · quick-add expenses · guest book. Deferred: push notifications, photo hosting, typing indicators, ratings.
